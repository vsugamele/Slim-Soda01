2. Copy — breakdown estrutural (4 blocos)

"Look at this. See that dent in my ankle? That's from my sock. It's 5pm. And no, I didn't eat a bag of chips. It's not sodium."

O que faz: (1) shows evidence (camera no tornozelo antes de subir pra face), (2) time-specificity ("5pm"), (3) preempt debunk ("I didn't eat chips" — antecipa o que viewer tá pensando). Viewer pensa "eu tenho isso EXATAMENTE assim" → pre-qualification instantânea sem disease claim.

Estrutura reusável: [Look at X]. [It's TIME]. [I didn't do OBVIOUS CAUSE]. [It's not X]. Compliance-safe: sock marks + 5pm são fatos observáveis.

O que faz: (1) anatomia real (lymphatic existe mas leigos não conhecem), (2) "body's drain" = metáfora acessível, (3) "sluggish after 40/50" = age-specific, (4) "different system" = reorienta categoria. Viewer ouve "lymphatic" e pensa "nunca ouvi falar, meu médico nunca mencionou" → buried cure conspiracy real (lymphatic health raramente discutido em consultas de 15min).

Estrutura reusável: Then I read about [SYSTEM]. It's basically [METAPHOR]. When it gets [VERB] after [AGE], [WHAT HAPPENS]. [OTHER SYSTEM] can't fix it. Compliance-safe: "lymphatic system" é anatomia, não claim.

Estrutura reusável: Researchers have studied [N] [ingredients]. [NAME 1], [NAME 2], [NAME 3], [NAME 4] — shown to [structure-function]. Not [bad category]. Different thing. Compliance-safe: "support healthy lymphatic flow" é structure-function FDA-permitido.
