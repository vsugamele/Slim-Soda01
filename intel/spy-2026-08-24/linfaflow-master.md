## 5. Compliance Watchlist 2026 (linhaflow-specific)

### 5.1 Meta (jul/2026 update — material!)
- ✅ **Claims-based review agora** (NÃO categoria): um ad pode passar se copy compliant mesmo sendo weight loss
- ✅ **Before/after imagery permitida** SE paired claim é compliant (ex: "I built this routine over a year" passa)
- ✅ **Supplements sem 18+ targeting default** — mas QUALQUER weight loss/gain claim traz 18+ de volta
- ✅ **Advantage+ catalog aberto pra supplement** vertical
- ❌ **Sensational/misleading health claims** — "cure", "guaranteed", specific timelines
- ❌ **Pinched-fat imagery** + negative self-perception framing = reject
- ❌ **Personal Attributes expanded** (jul/2026): indirect implications banidas (ex: "if you're struggling with X" = ban; "many people experience X" = ok)

### 5.2 FTC (Federal Trade Commission) — aplicado a TODOS canais
- **FTC** jul/2026 guidance: "advertisers must have adequate substantiation for all objective product claims"
- **FTC + FDA split**: FDA rotula structure-function (DSHEA), FTC questiona o substantiation (não o tipo)
- **Implicação**: "supports lymphatic flow" requer evidence file (não clinical trial mas estudos de herbs)
- **Health claims ≠ structure-function claims** — qualquer claim "reduces risk of disease X" = drug claim = FDA approved only

### 5.3 Linfaflow-specific red lines (NÃO USAR)
- ❌ **"Treats lymphedema"** (lymphedema é disease, drug claim)
- ❌ **"Cures edema"** (disease claim)
- ❌ **"Drains lymphatic system"** (medical claim — "supports lymphatic function" é OK)
- ❌ **"You have lymphedema"** (Personal Attributes Meta ban)
- ❌ **"FDA approved"** (supps não são)
- ❌ **"Lose 20 lbs in 14 days"** (Meta health policy express ban)
- ❌ **"90,000 women/men are using"** (fabricated social proof — red line do user)
- ❌ **"548% reduction"** (fabricated stat — Barchart coverage)
- ❌ **"Cures cancer"** / **"Reverses diabetes"** (express FDA ban)
- ❌ **"Stop your diuretic"** (medical advice — Linfaflow canon explicit hard rule: "Talk to your doctor")

### 5.4 Linfaflow OK language (use)
- ✅ "Supports lymphatic function" / "Supports lymphatic flow"
- ✅ "Helps maintain healthy fluid balance"
- ✅ "May help reduce occasional water retention"
- ✅ "Promotes natural detoxification"
- ✅ "4 botanicals traditionally used to support lymphatic health"
- ✅ "Consult your doctor" (sempre)
- ✅ "These statements have not been evaluated by the FDA. This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease."
