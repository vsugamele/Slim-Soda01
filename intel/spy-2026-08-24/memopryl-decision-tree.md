## Pergunta 6 — Qual é o compliance check?

### 6.1 Hard red lines (NUNCA — verify em todo criativo)

```
❌ "Improves memory" (verbatim ou paraphrase) — Prevagen injunction
❌ "Clinically shown to improve memory" — Prevagen + Neuriva
❌ "Within 90 days" (ou qualquer time-bound) — Prevagen
❌ "Reduces memory problems associated with aging" — Prevagen
❌ "Sharper mind, clearer thinking" (como claim) — Prevagen
❌ "Proven" / "Backed by science" — Neuriva $8M
❌ "Cures Alzheimer's" / "Treats dementia" / "Prevents memory loss" — FDA + FTC
❌ "FDA approved" (supps não são approved) — Meta 2026
❌ Deepfake Bill Gates / Bruce Willis / Anderson Cooper / Oprah — RED LINE absoluta
❌ "100% guaranteed results" — Meta 2026 + FTC
```

### 6.2 Compliance-safe language (USE)

```
✅ "Supports memory function" — FDA structure-function
✅ "May help with memory" — hedged structure-function
✅ "Clinically tested" (NÃO "shown" / "proven") — Neuriva settlement language
✅ "Research summary" — MemoPryl av8 framework
✅ "Paraphrasing Nature 2025" — distancing from endorsement
✅ "Non-clinical dose" (5mg lithium) — drug claim mitigation
✅ "May support healthy cognitive aging" — replaces "prevents memory loss"
✅ "Individual results may vary" — risk reversal boilerplate
✅ "Consult your physician" — lithium + statins interaction risk
✅ "Not a cure. Not a treatment." — disclaimer embutido (MemoPryl av3 pattern)
```

### 6.3 Boilerplate obrigatório em TODA LP

```
[FDA DISCLAIMER]
These statements have not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration.
This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.

[INDIVIDUAL RESULTS]
Individual results may vary.

[CONSULT]
Consult your physician before beginning any supplement, especially if you are
pregnant, nursing, taking medication, or have a medical condition.

[AGE]
Not intended for individuals under 18.

[AI LABEL — se aplicável]
This ad contains AI-generated content.
```
