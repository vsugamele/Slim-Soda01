## 1. PERSONA → ÂNGULO (matriz de decisão)

### P1. Mulher 35-55 working/professional
- **Quando atacar**: weekday 6am-9am + 5pm-9pm (peak edema)
- **Dor central**: "I'm doing everything right — eat clean, exercise, sleep 7h — but I'm STILL bloated by 5pm"
- **Ângulos validados** (em ordem de prioridade):
  1. **"5pm Ankle Check"** (time-specificity, ADV4 validado) — `Why your socks leave deeper marks by 5pm (it's not sodium)`
  2. **"Why you're still bloated even when you eat clean"** (curiosity + contradiction)
  3. **"Puffy face by morning"** (visible evidence)
  4. **"The 4 symptoms of slow lymphatic flow"** (educational)

### P2. Mulher 45-65 wellness-curious / blue zone
- **Quando atacar**: weekend morning + weekday evening
- **Dor central**: "I've heard about Sardinia / Ikaria / Okinawa. What's their secret?"
- **Ângulos validados**:
  1. **"Sardinian women live past 100. 4 botanicals in their morning water."** (exotic atemporal, NÃO "IN 1958")
  2. **"What Amazonian grandmothers have done for lymphatic health"** (BR provenance)
  3. **"On a Greek island, women over 80 have the lowest rates of swelling. Researchers found why."**
  4. **"Mediterranean lymphatic ritual — 4 botanicals, 30 seconds"**

### P3. Mulher 55-70+ menopausal
- **Quando atacar**: weekday morning + evening
- **Dor central**: "I gained 7 lbs in my 60s and NOTHING comes off. Not fat, nothing budges."
- **Ângulos validados**:
  1. **"The 7 lbs that won't come off in your 60s isn't fat — it's stagnant lymph"** (compliance OK: "stagnant lymph" é structure-function, "isn't fat" é contraste)
  2. **"Why menopause belly won't go away — and what your doctor never explained"**
  3. **"After 60, your lymphatic system slows by 40%. Here's what that looks like."**

### P4. Mulher 35-60 travel / business
- **Quando atacar**: 24-48h before/after flight (peak situational)
- **Dor central**: "After a 6-hour flight, my ankles are swollen for 2 days"
- **Ângulos validados**:
  1. **"After a 6-hour flight, why your ankles don't bounce back for 2 days"** (time-specificity, compliance-friendly)
  2. **"3 things every frequent flyer should do after landing"** (educational)
  3. **"I added 4 drops before takeoff. Here's what happened when I landed."**
  4. **"Travel day ritual: 4 botanicals, 30 seconds, no compression socks"**

### P5. Mulher 50-70+ sister/vein procedure (Margaret Story)
- **Quando atacar**: weekday evening + Sunday morning
- **Dor central**: "My sister had a routine vein procedure. She died of a blood clot 4 weeks later. She was 62."
- **Ângulo flagship** (Linfaflow canon):
  - **"My older sister had a routine vein procedure. She died of a blood clot 4 weeks later. She was 62."**
  - Followed by: 30-second morning ritual with 4 plant extracts (Break / Sweep / Activate / Purify)
  - Stats: Week 1 tightness eased / Week 2 stairs no heart pound / Week 3 ankle dent test / Week 4 shoes fit at 6pm / Month 2 BP low 120s, surgery cancelled.
- **LP type**: VSL Filemon E3 long-form (único formato que faz jus à Margaret Story)

### P6. Homem 50-70+ dad bod (Cluster 3 emergente)
- **Quando atacar**: weekday morning + evening
- **Dor central**: "I'm 60. My belly is bigger than my dad's at 75. Something's not right."
- **Ângulos Meta-safe** (NÃO replicar Lymph Phoria profanity):
  1. **"Man 60 here. 4 botanicals. 30-second morning ritual. Day 60: belt 2 holes tighter."**
  2. **"After 50, your lymphatic system slows down. Your body holds onto everything. Here's what 4 botanicals do."**
  3. **"I added 4 drops to my morning water for 60 days. The difference showed in the mirror, not the scale."**
- **⚠️ Compliance**:
  - ZERO profanity (Meta reject)
  - ZERO "90,000 men" / fabricated social proof
  - ZERO weight loss measurement (trigger 18+ targeting Meta jul/2026)
  - ZERO "your lymphatic system is clogged shut" (disease claim)
  - ✅ "supports healthy lymphatic flow" + "may help" + visible relatability ("belt 2 holes")

### P7. Lymphedema-specific (NÃO ATACAR — red line)
- **Por quê NÃO**: lymphedema é disease, "treats lymphedema" = drug claim, "you have lymphedema" = Personal Attributes ban
- **Alternativa**: usar "supports lymphatic function" + "may help reduce occasional water retention" + "consult your doctor"
- **Linfaflow canon hard rule**: SEMPRE "Talk to your doctor" — copy canônica nunca diz "stop your diuretic"

## 2. ÂNGULO → FRAMEWORK DE COPY (qual estrutura usar)

### Framework A: **UGC iPhone 60-90s** (P1, P4, P6 — peer first-person)
**Pattern (adv4 validado)**:
- **Scene 1 (0-3s)**: visible evidence (sock indent, swollen ankle, puffy face) + time-specificity + debunk
- **Scene 2 (3-18s)**: relatable setup ("I used to think it was X — I cut X for 3 months — still swollen")
- **Scene 3 (18-50s)**: anatomy education ("lymphatic system = body's drain, sluggish after 40/50")
- **Scene 4 (50-80s)**: 4 named botanicals + mechanism + "not a diuretic" + "supports healthy flow"
- **Scene 5 (80-90s)**: transformation soft ("my socks don't leave marks anymore") + low-friction CTA ("watch the 30-second morning ritual")

**Tom**: first-person, peer, conversational, NO production value
**Compliance**: sock marks, time of day, visible symptoms = factual; "supports" + "may help" = structure-function
**Replicable pra**: P1 (5pm), P4 (post-flight), P6 (male 60+)

### Framework B: **VSL Filemon E3 6 blocks** (P2, P5 — long-form)
**Pattern** (Method E3 do Filemon):
- **Block 1 — Hook (0-3min)**: "Is everything you've been told about X a lie?" (P5 Margaret) OU "What Sardinian women know about lymphatic health" (P2)
- **Block 2 — Story (3-8min)**: Margaret funeral opening (P5) OU Sardinian centenarian story (P2)
- **Block 3 — Mechanism (8-18min)**: 4 herbs + lymphatic system education + "not a diuretic"
- **Block 4 — Evidence (18-25min)**: clinical studies, real research, "researchers have been studying this for years"
- **Block 5 — Offer (25-32min)**: 3-tier pricing (1-bottle / 3-bottle / 6-bottle) + bonus digital guides
- **Block 6 — Close (32-40min)**: 60-day MBG + "talk to your doctor" + scarcity overlay

**Tom**: calm, authoritative, story-driven, peer trust
**Compliance**: "supports" + "may help" + "researchers studying" (sem "FDA hide" / "forbidden enzyme" — red lines)
**Replicable pra**: P2 (wellness), P5 (Margaret canon)

### Framework C: **Advertorial blog → VSL mid-form** (P3 menopause, P4 travel)
**Pattern**:
- **Article (3-5min read)**: "5 things every woman over 60 should know about her lymphatic system" (P3) OU "Why frequent flyers' ankles swell for 2 days after landing" (P4)
- **Inline CTA**: "Watch the 4-minute video"
- **VSL mid-form (4-8min)**: condensed Filemon E3 (4 blocks em vez de 6)
- **Order form**: 3-tier pricing

**Tom**: editorial, factual, educational
**Compliance**: educational structure-function + "consult your doctor" + age-specific
**Replicable pra**: P3, P4, men-focused variant

### Framework D: **Carrossel educativo 5-7 cards** (universal — P1, P2, P3, P4, P6)
**Pattern**:
- **Card 1**: hook visual + symptom question
- **Card 2**: visible evidence (anatomy diagram ou sock mark)
- **Card 3**: 1 cause (lymphatic system) + debunk ("not sodium", "not diet")
- **Card 4**: 4 named botanicals (cleavers, red clover, stillingia, prickly ash)
- **Card 5**: 30-second ritual steps
- **Card 6**: timeline (week 1-2-3-4, month 2)
- **Card 7**: CTA + Linfaflow 4-pillar proof (Made in USA, FDA-registered, 3rd-party tested, 60-day MBG)

**Tom**: educational, calm, editorial
**Compliance**: factual botanical names + "supports" + visual evidences are descriptive
**Replicable pra**: P1, P2, P3, P4, P6 — cluster mais reutilizável

### Framework E: **Partnership Ad (creator licensed)** (P1, P3 — Meta 2026 scale)
**Pattern**:
- Creator licensed via Meta Partnership Ads (disclosure obrigatório)
- Creator fala first-person, "I added X to my morning"

- **Compliance**: creator deve estar ciente que é partnership, FTC disclosure visível
**Replicable pra**: P1 (5pm), P3 (menopause)
