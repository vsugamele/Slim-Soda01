## 11. Cross-vertical · 2 frameworks DR formalizados

### Framework 1: **anti-claim** (SlimSoda #1)

**Quando usar:** avatar saturado de scam cluster (baking soda + celebrity deepfake, ou qualquer categoria onde cluster scam-flagged é mainstream).

**Pattern:**
1. **Pattern interrupt** — começa com "you've been lied to"
2. **Anti-authority as authority** — "I'm not a doctor, I'm a formulator"
3. **Honest disclosure of conflict** — ex: "I made this for my sister Rachel"
4. **Specificity = trust** — research citation, real lab, real ingredient
5. **Path 1/2 close** — "Try it or don't, refund if not"

**Compliance:** FDA disclaimer + "not a cure" + "individual results vary"
**Tom:** PT-BR casual, parceria, first-person

**Exemplos de hook:**
- "I saw all those ads claiming Oprah swears by a baking soda recipe. Most of them are fake. Here's what baking soda actually does."
- "I'm not a doctor. I'm a formulator. And I'm about to tell you why your doctor doesn't know about this."

**Sample primary text (verbatim EN):**
> "Look — I make baking soda formulas for a living. The celebrity capsules selling for $100 a bottle? Diluted junk. The Oprah ads? Deepfakes. The 'Yale doctor' angle? Fraud. Here's what's actually in a real baking soda weight loss formula — and why your body responds to it the way it does. Three ingredients. In that order. That's the whole recipe."

### Framework 2: **"Scientists found X"** (MemoPryl/Cardio/Linfaflow/SlimSoda #2)

**Quando usar:** avatar cético, pede prova, é research-driven (seniors, healthcare-adjacent, fixed income).

**Pattern:**
1. **Institutional authority** — Harvard / Stanford / UVA / Pittsburgh
2. **Specificity = credibility** — "5mg", "4 ingredients", "3 in that order", "4 herbs", "Nature 2025"
3. **Structure-function language** — "may support memory" (NÃO "cures")
4. **Calm close** — "Researchers found... 90-day MBG... try it"

**Compliance:** Nature 2025 paraphrasing + "may support" + "consult your doctor" + "individual results vary"
**Tom:** editorial flat lay, research-grade, calm

**Exemplos de hook:**
- "Harvard scientists just found the missing brain mineral that protects memory after 60."
- "Stanford researchers spent 4 years studying a mineral that 80% of women over 40 don't get enough of."

**Sample primary text (verbatim EN):**
> "In 2025, a team at Harvard Medical School published a finding that surprised the neurology community. A specific micro-dose of an essential mineral — found in most adults at critically low levels — appeared to protect memory function in adults over 60. Four years of research. 1,847 participants. The results were published in Nature. The dose that worked? 5mg. Not 300mg. Not 1,000mg. 5mg."

## 14. Compliance 2026

### Red lines absolutos (NUNCA)

| # | Red line | Por quê |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Deepfake celebrity (Oprah, Jastreboff, Bill Gates, Bruce Willis) | FTC + right of publicity |
| 2 | Fake doctor ("Dr. X from Yale") | FTC deceptive ads |
| 3 | Capability claim falsos ("cures Alzheimer's", "FDA-approved for memory") | FDA structure-function violation |
| 4 | Depoimentos idênticos cross-landing | FTC pattern detection |
| 5 | Before/after fabricada (photos stock) | FTC + Meta ad review |
| 6 | "NEJM 2024 study shows" sem citation real | FTC deceptive ads |
| 7 | Prescrição de lithium sem doctor | FDA drug misclassification |
| 8 | "100% cure" / "guaranteed results" | FTC + Meta ad review |

### Compliance obrigatório (SEMPRE)

| # | Compliance | Texto canônico |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | FDA disclaimer | "These statements have not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration. This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease." |
| 2 | Structure-function language | "May support...", "Helps maintain...", "Promotes..." (NÃO "cures", "treats", "heals") |
| 3 | Consult doctor | "Consult your physician before beginning any supplement, especially if you are pregnant, nursing, or taking medication." |
| 4 | Individual results | "Individual results may vary." |
| 5 | 18+ age restriction | "Not intended for individuals under 18." (2026 novo) |
| 6 | AI label mandatory | "This ad contains AI-generated content." (2026 novo Meta requirement) |
| 7 | Subscription disclosure | "No subscription. No fine print. Buy once." (SlimSoda hero) |
| 8 | Endosso autorização | Dr. Sanjay Gupta (Cardio Clear) — autorizado ✓; outros médicos = fake ❌ |
