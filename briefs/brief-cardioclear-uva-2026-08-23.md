## 3. Mecanismos DR (6 levers)

1. **Pre-qualification (institucional):** "UVA Health + peer-reviewed cardiovascular research" filtra viewer que VALORA research. Quem clica já tá 50% vendido no ângulo "quero o que UVA descobriu".
2. **Authority transfer (research + doctor → product):** Viewer transfere trust de **UVA (institution) + Dr. Sanjay Gupta (brand owner/authorized)** pro Cardio Clear. Não confia no produto — confia no médico e na research.
3. **Buried cure conspiracy (atacar mito do colesterol):** "The real driver isn't cholesterol — it's vascular inflammation. That's why statins don't fix everything." Ataca mito sem citar marca. Stain fear → "real driver revelation" → alternative.
4. **Low-friction CTA (read, not watch):** "Read the 30-second research summary" — 60-75+ women preferem **text sobre video** pra conteúdo técnico (heart health = revisar com calma, consultar filha).
5. **Aspirational identity (preservação):** "Protect your heart after 60" — preservação ("viva e ativa pros netos"), não melhoria ("boost athletic performance").
6. **Specificity = credibility (4 named ingredients é o herói):** **4 ingredientes named** (não "proprietary blend" — genérico = scam). Cada um targeting pathway: arterial stiffness, inflammation, endothelial function, circulation. 4-in-1 = specificity que mata ceticismo.

## 6. Compliance checklist

### ❌ NÃO fazer (red lines)

- ❌ "cures heart disease" / "treats hypertension" / "lowers blood pressure" / "replaces statins" (FDA drug territory)
- ❌ Comparar com statins específicos (Lipitor, Crestor) — só atacar INDÚSTRIA
- ❌ "before/after" de blood pressure (impossível medir + Meta 2026 ban)
- ❌ Deepfake de Dr. Sanjay Gupta ou cardiologist real (right of publicity + FTC 2024)
- ❌ Atacar marca específica (Pfizer, AstraZeneca, etc)
- ❌ Depoimentos "I was on statins, now I'm off" (off-label claim)
- ❌ "Doctor recommended" sem especificação (FTC 2024 mass warning)
- ❌ Foto real não autorizada de Dr. Sanjay Gupta

### ✅ DEVE ter (compliance + trust)

- ✅ "May support cardiovascular health" (structure-function, FDA-safe)
- ✅ "4 named ingredients, not a proprietary blend" (transparência)
- ✅ "Paraphrasing UVA Health research" (research summary, não endorsement)
- ✅ "Built by Dr. Sanjay Gupta, brand owner" (autorização explícita)
- ✅ FDA disclaimer: **"These statements have not been evaluated by the FDA. This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease."**
- ✅ Source citation: "Source: UVA Health cardiovascular research (paraphrased)"
- ✅ "This is not a cure. This is a structure-function summary." (disclaimer no ad)
- ✅ Ilustração/silhueta ou ator similar + AI label se avatar sintético (Meta 2026) + 18+ age restriction + Disclosure #ad
