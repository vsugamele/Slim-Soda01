Path 1/2 Variants · 5 Frameworks

Path 1/2 decision frame é o close estrutural de QUALQUER copy DR. 5 variantes do mesmo framework: Loss Aversion · Identity · Cost · Time · Emotion. Cada variante tem 2-3 exemplos reais (SlimSoda/Cardio/Linfozen). Cross-project: replicável pra qualquer vertical com close binário.

🎯 Por que Path 1/2 funciona: cérebro humano toma decisões binárias mais rápido que ternárias. Path 1 = custo de não agir (loss frame). Path 2 = benefício de agir (gain frame). "Most Popular" badge no Path 2 empurra 70%+ do tráfego pra offer. Cross-vertical: Maria 47 v3 (SlimSoda), advertorial-uva-v1 (Cardio), advertorial-lipedema-v1 (Linfozen) todas usam Path 1/2.
1

Loss Aversion Path

"If you do nothing" vs "If you start today" · Kahneman Tversky · Loss > Gain

Mecanismo psicológico: Kahneman-Tversky prospect theory. Loss averse ~2x mais que gain seeking. Path 1 = quantificar o loss. Path 2 = quantificar o gain.

Path 1 · "If You Do Nothing"

Another Monday. Another 6 months. Another photo you're not in. Another 90 days of cement hardening. Another $1,200-$3,600 in drainage that relieves for 24 hours. Another 5pm sensation.

Path 2 · "If You Start Today" ⭐ Most Popular

The 4-Ingredient Method. 30 seconds. Wake. Protect. Repair. Flip. The next 90 days could be the 90 days that change everything. The first morning you button your pants at 3pm without thinking about it.

Quando usar: cold traffic, high-intent, "Maria Soco" mode. Funciona em TODOS os níveis L1-L4. L1 suavizar ("may"), L4 intensificar ("90 days of damage").

2

Identity Path

"Stay the woman who..." vs "Become the woman who..." · Identity lock

Mecanismo psicológico: Identity-based habits (James Clear). Decisões alinhadas com a identidade que a pessoa quer ter. Path 1 = preservar a identidade atual. Path 2 = abraçar a identidade futura.

Path 1 · "Stay the Woman Who..."

Stay the woman who finishes every diet, then regains. Stay the woman who avoids mirrors at 5pm. Stay the woman who tells herself "it's just age" while her legs swell. Stay the woman whose daughter keeps trying to give her supplements.

Path 2 · "Become the Woman Who..." ⭐ Most Popular

Become the woman whose granddaughter learns to ride a bike. Become the woman who wears skirts to work again. Become the woman who tried the protocol and saw BP go from 170/110 to 120/80. Become the woman who finally has a quiet kitchen conversation that changes everything.

Quando usar: avatar com identity cost claro (perdeu algo importante). Patricia 67 (Cardio) — granddaughter. Lisa 39 (Linfozen) — skirts to work. Susan P. (SlimSoda) — daughter's wedding.

3

Cost Path

"$20K vs $147" · Money anchor · Procedural vs natural

Mecanismo psicológico: money anchor. Comparação de preço alto (procedimento invasivo) vs preço real (suplemento). Funciona em QUALQUER vertical com alternativa cara (lipo, injeção, doctor visit, therapist).

Path 1 · "The Expensive Way"

Twenty-thousand-dollar liposuction, where the fat just comes back. $1,000 a month on injections that mask the symptom. $300 a week on manual drainage that relieves for 24 hours. $5,000-$20,000 in procedures that carry risk. The industry is happy to take your money.

Path 2 · "The Natural Way" ⭐ Most Popular

Three ingredients from one Blue Zone. 30 seconds a day. $59.99 for 30 days. $147 for 90 days. $294 for the full 180-day protocol. 1% the cost of the alternative. 90-day empty-bottle guarantee. Your only risk is doing nothing.

Quando usar: avatar já tentou alternativa cara (lipo, injeção, drenagem) sem sucesso. OU avatar "investigando opções" antes de decidir. Cross-vertical: Lipfozen $20K lipo, Cardio $20K lipo, SlimSoda $1K/mo injeções, Linfaflow $300/semana drenagem.

4

Time Path

"Another 6 months" vs "The next 90 days" · Temporal anchor

Mecanismo psicológico: temporal framing. Path 1 = tempo perdido. Path 2 = tempo que vem. Funciona especialmente pra avatares established que perderam tempo.

Path 1 · "Another 6 Months"

Another 6 months of trying everything that doesn't work. Another summer in a cover-up. Another closet where you get dressed so he won't see. Another 6 months of telling your daughter "I just need to try harder." Another 6 months of food noise you didn't ask for.

Path 2 · "The Next 90 Days" ⭐ Most Popular

The next 90 days could be the 90 days that change everything. Wake. Protect. Repair. Flip. Use the full protocol. 30 seconds a day. The first morning your hands don't ache. The first afternoon you don't undo your top button. The first evening you don't need the drainage.

Quando usar: avatar "established" (8+ anos tentando, frustration alta). Maria 47 (SlimSoda) — 8 anos trying. Patricia 67 (Cardio) — 8 anos Lisinopril. Lisa 39 (Linfozen) — 16 anos. Margaret 62 (Linfaflow) — 6 anos.

5

Emotion Path

"Still afraid" vs "Finally hopeful" · Emotional lever

Mecanismo psicológico: emotional contrast. Path 1 = estado emocional negativo (medo, raiva, vergonha). Path 2 = estado emocional positivo (esperança, alívio, orgulho).

Path 1 · "Still Afraid"

Still afraid of the next cardiologist appointment. Still afraid to step on the scale. Still afraid your daughter will inherit this. Still afraid the doctor is right that 'it's just age.' Still afraid to wear shorts. Still afraid to look in the mirror at 5pm. Still afraid of the next procedure.

Path 2 · "Finally Hopeful" ⭐ Most Popular

Finally hopeful when the doctor says your pressure is stabilizing. Finally hopeful when you button your pants without trying. Finally hopeful when you wear the dress you gave up on. Finally hopeful when the food noise quiets. Finally hopeful when the 5pm heaviness doesn't come.

Quando usar: avatar exausto (já tentou tudo, "tired of being sold to"). Funciona melhor como complemento das outras 4 variantes, não isolado. Maria Quiet mode = Emotion Path + Time Path = combo mais forte.

🔗 Cross-project: como aplicar a QUALQUER copy DR

  1. Identifica o avatar (frustrated established / cold high-intent / warm exhausted / proxy-buy / post-event)
  2. Escolhe 2-3 variants baseado no decision matrix acima
  3. Escreve Path 1 (loss frame) com emoção concreta ("another 6 months", "$20K", "still afraid", "stay the woman")
  4. Escreve Path 2 (gain frame) com emoção concreta + Most Popular badge
  5. CTA final ecoa o Path 2 ("Start the 90-day protocol" / "Begin the 30-second ritual" / "Try the 4-ingredient method")
  6. Testa em volume (Bifi P1 com 5 concepts × 3 texts usando mixes diferentes)
  7. Mede qual combo converte (Hook Performance Tracker)
