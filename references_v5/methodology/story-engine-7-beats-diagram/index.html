Story-Engine 7 Beats · Diagrama Visual

Os 7 beats canônicos de qualquer copy DR (LP, VSL, advertorial, chat). Framework replicável cross-vertical: Lead → Bridge → Invalidate → Mechanism → Proof → Action → Close. Cada beat tem: função narrativa, técnica psicológica, exemplo verbatim, red line check.

🎯 Por que 7 beats funciona: copy sem framework vira rant. Copy com framework vira jornada. Lead captura atenção em 3s. Bridge promete "stay for X minutos". Invalidate destrói solução anterior. Mechanism instala novo modelo mental. Proof valida com testemunho. Action ritualiza uso. Close fecha com offer binário (Path 1/2). Cross-project: os 7 beats aparecem em TUDO (Maria 47 v3 SlimSoda, advertorial-uva-v1 Cardio, advertorial-lipedema-v1 Linfozen, 3 chat-x1 briefings).

🎯 Os 7 beats em detalhe (com verbatim por beat)

1

LEAD · Hook · 3s

Função: capturar atenção em 3 segundos. Pattern interrupt. Curiosity gap aberto. Loss tease.

Técnica psicológica: cérebro humano tem 3s de attention span. Lead precisa abrir um loop ("o que vem depois?"). Mix de 2-3 tipos de urgência fabricada (ver urgency-7-types).

"Researchers at UVA Just Discovered the Real Driver of High Blood Pressure After 60 — And It's Not Cholesterol."

SlimSoda: "For eight years, I tried everything." · Cardio: "Mr. Roberts, 64, didn't make it to his granddaughter's recital." · Linfozen: "If your leg looks like this... Or this... Maybe it's not fat."

2

BRIDGE · Commit · 30s

Função: pedir commitment de tempo. Sunk cost. "Stay for X minutos, then decide."

Técnica psicológica: sunk cost + commitment device. Pessoa que ouviu 30s tem 3× mais chance de chegar ao close do que pessoa que acabou de chegar.

"Stay with me for 12 minutes. If you have ever been the woman blaming her body for failing at something you were not even doing right, this is for you."

Cross-vertical: 12 min (SlimSoda) · 30s (Linfozen ritual) · 17s (Linfozen teaser) · "I'm about to tell you something" (Cardio).

3

INVALIDATE · Destroy old · 1m

Função: destruir modelo mental anterior. 4 invalidações. Old way = broken.

Técnica psicológica: criar cognitive dissonance. Reader precisa de novo modelo pra resolver. Sem invalidar, mechanism não instala.

"Diet won't fix it. Exercise won't fix it. Ozempic won't fix it. Twenty-thousand-dollar liposuction won't fix it. Three ingredients. Ten seconds a day. That's the whole ritual."

SlimSoda: 3 attempts (water pills / ACV / baking soda alone) + "the order is what matters" · Cardio: "no statin dissolves cement" · Linfozen: 4 invalidações + conspiracy ($4B industry).

4

MECHANISM · Reveal · 2m

Função: instalar novo modelo mental. 3-4 ingredientes named. Order matters. "Miss one, and the whole thing collapses."

Técnica psicológica: new mental model + sequence dependency. Reader precisa aprender a ordem pra ser digno do produto.

"Wake. Protect. Repair. Flip. The order is the mechanism. Skip one, and the whole thing weakens. This is what the viral videos leave out — and what most 'berberine + baking soda' recipes on Amazon miss entirely."

SlimSoda: Wake (baking soda) → Protect (gingerol) → Repair (NAD+) → Flip (berberine) · Cardio: Bitter Honey (dissolve fibrin) → Cagnulari (soften) → Botanical (inflammation) → Mineral (stabilize) · Linfozen: Bromelain (dissolve capsule) → Dandelion (lymphatic) → Cleavers (anti-inflammatory).

5

PROOF · Validate · 1.5m

Função: validar mechanism com testemunho + stat. 3 testemunhos. Survivor contrast (vs mortality beat 2 ou 3).

Técnica psicológica: social proof + survivor bias. Pessoa que sobreviveu é mais convincente que pessoa que curou (porque "no cure" é aceito).

"Patricia R., 67 years, Tampa, FL. 8 years on Lisinopril. After 6 weeks: 120/80. She cried in her doctor's office. I was completely wrong about the supplements my daughter kept trying to give me."

Cross-vertical: Patricia 67 (Cardio) · Lisa 39 (Linfozen) · Jessica 51 (Linfaflow) · Karen 52 RN (Linfozen skeptic MD converted).

6

ACTION · Ritual · 1m

Função: ritualizar uso. 3-3-2 cadence. "X seconds. Y minutes. Z times a day." Commitment action.

Técnica psicológica: implementation intention (Gollwitzer). Específico + situational = 2-3× mais adherence que vago.

"Two capsules. Before coffee. Before breakfast. 30 seconds. That's the whole ritual. 100% compatible with Losartan, Lisinopril, and Amlodipine. Always talk to your doctor before changing your routine."

SlimSoda: "One scoop. One glass. Three seconds." · Cardio: "Two capsules. Before coffee. 30 seconds." · Linfozen: "Ten seconds. Every morning. In water. Before coffee." · Linfaflow: "4 drops under tongue. 30 seconds hold. 4-stage: Break/Sweep/Activate/Purify."

7

CLOSE · Path 1/2 · 30s

Função: fechar com decisão binária. Path 1 = loss frame. Path 2 = gain frame + Most Popular.

Técnica psicológica: binary choice (Kahneman) + loss aversion. 70%+ do tráfego vai pro Path 2 (Most Popular badge).

"Path 1: I'm already on medication. Single bottle $49, start with 30-day supply. Path 2: I'm done waiting for the pill to work. 6 bottles $294, 90-day protocol with empty-bottle money-back guarantee. Most Popular."

Cross-vertical: ver path-decision-variants para 5 frameworks diferentes (Loss/Identity/Cost/Time/Emotion).

📐 Como aplicar (workflow)

  1. Antes de escrever QUALQUER copy DR, abre esse diagrama
  2. Escolhe archetypal stories (story-archetypal-library) por beat
  3. Combina com Path 1/2 variants (path-decision-variants) no close (beat 7)
  4. Mix urgency types (urgency-7-types) por beat (especialmente beat 1 lead + beat 3 invalidate)
  5. Valida compliance (urgency-compliance-guide) por beat antes de ship
  6. Timing: 6-8 min total (3s + 30s + 1m + 2m + 1.5m + 1m + 30s). Respeitar.

🔗 Cross-project: como aplicar a QUALQUER copy DR

  1. Lead (beat 1) é universal — toda copy abre com hook. Mix 2-3 urgency types.
  2. Bridge (beat 2) é commitment — pedir tempo. "Stay for X min" / "I'm about to tell you".
  3. Invalidate (beat 3) é destruction — 4 invalidações ou 1 narrative arc (Maria 47 = 8 anos tentativas).
  4. Mechanism (beat 4) é reveal — 3-4 ingredientes named, sequence matters.
  5. Proof (beat 5) é validation — 3 testemunhos (survivor contrast) + 3 stats (canon).
  6. Action (beat 6) é ritual — 3-3-2 cadence + compatibility + compliance call.
  7. Close (beat 7) é decision — Path 1/2 binary choice + Most Popular badge + MBG.
