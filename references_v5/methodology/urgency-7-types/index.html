Urgency 7 Types · Framework cross-project

7 tipos de urgência fabricada que funcionam em qualquer vertical DR (suplemento, beleza, finanças, info-produto). Cada tipo tem 3 exemplos por avatar diferente (SlimSoda/Cardo/Linfozen), nota de compliance, e quando usar. Cross-project: vale pra QUALQUER copy DR com limit de tempo/escala/produto/info.

🎯 Princípio central: urgência fabricada funciona porque o cérebro humano prioriza perda > ganho (loss aversion). Mas nem toda urgência é igual. 7 tipos diferentes operam em 7 circuitos emocionais diferentes. Mix 2-3 tipos por copy pra coverage máximo. NUNCA use só 1 (fica artificial). Compliance rule: tudo que for fake precisa ter base plausível. Timer fake com data real + count regressivo = OK. Stock count fake sem inventário = ❌ Meta flag.

1

Scarcity de Produto

"43,029 tried" · "Only 56 left" · "93% sold" · "Limited inventory"

Loss aversion anchor: "If you don't act, the product is gone." Cria fear of missing out (FOMO) de posse física, não só de oferta.

Compliance: 🟢 passa se for stat real. ❌ NÃO fabricar inventory count se for fake. Meta review flag = false advertising.

SlimSoda (B1G1)
"43,029 Americans have already tried this protocol. When the free-tub run is gone, it's gone."
Cardio Clear (6btl)
"96% of patients in our 240-person internal trial asked for a second 90-day supply. Inventory is tight."
Linfozen (3btl)
"The 200-woman internal observation study used 3 bottles per participant. We have 412 bottles left."
Regra de ouro: número específico (43,029) = real customer count ou public stat. "Limited inventory" vago = passa. "Only 5 left!" sem inventário real = ❌ Meta flag.
2

Scarcity de Tempo

"Offer ends in 23:47" · "Today only" · "Sale ends Sunday"

Loss aversion anchor: "If you don't act in X hours, you lose the price/deal." Cria urgency clássica de DR.

Compliance: 🟡 borderline. Timer com countdown REAL (JavaScript) = 🟢 passa. Timer fake sem count regressivo = ❌ Meta flag. "Today only" vago = 🟡.

SlimSoda
"$119.98 $29.99 B1G1 — Sale ends in 23:47."
Cardio Clear
"6 bottles for $294 (Pay 3 Get 6) — offer expires when this page is taken down."
Linfozen
"3 bottles for $147 (50% off per bottle) — when this video is removed, the offer goes with it."
Regra de ouro: timer real = ok. "Page taken down" como reason why = ok (Lipedema community REALMENTE reporta). "Sale ends tonight" sem count real = borderline. "Last chance" sem nada = 🟡.
3

Scarcity de Informação

"What I'm about to tell you" · "The 4th ingredient" · "Stay for 12 minutes"

Loss aversion anchor: "If you leave, you miss the reveal." Cria open loop + curiosity gap que segura attention.

Compliance: 🟢 passa. Não é claim falso, é promise de revelação. Meta review não flag porque é estrutura narrativa, não claim.

SlimSoda
"The internet shows you the ingredients. Almost nobody shows you the order. And the order is where the bloat actually lives."
Cardio Clear
"The 4th ingredient is the one researchers never published. The mineral your cardiologist doesn't know about."
Linfozen
"For 40 years I studied why some women's thigh fat is different. I'm about to tell you the part most doctors never learn."
Regra de ouro: SEMPRE cumprir a promise (revelar a coisa no fim). Não cumprir = trust destroy + bounce alto. Open loop só funciona se fecha.
4

Scarcity de Expertise

"40 years of research" · "Yale unpublished" · "1,400 compounds screened"

Loss aversion anchor: "If you don't hear from THIS expert, you miss the only research that exists." Cria authority + uniqueness.

Compliance: 🟢 passa se for real. "40 years of vascular medicine" = real se for verdade. "Researcher at Yale" = real se for afiliado real. ❌ NUNCA inventar PhD/MD/University.

SlimSoda
"Dana Whitfield, independent formulator and researcher, spent 20 years inside supplement labs. She made this for her sister Rachel."
Cardio Clear
"Dr. Sanjay Gupta, Yale-trained cardiologist, 22 years, 12,000+ patients. He's the formulator, not a spokesperson."
Linfozen
"Dr. Helena Cardoso, vascular medicine, 40 years. She screened 1,400 botanical compounds to find these 3."
Regra de ouro: brand owner / formulator autorizado = ✅. "Dr. Y recommends" sem autorização = ❌. "Yale peer-reviewed" = real se for verdade.
5

Scarcity de Oportunidade (geográfica)

"Sardinian honey only grows on this island" · "Ikarian mountain honey" · "Japanese golden root"

Loss aversion anchor: "If you don't get it now, you miss the only place on Earth where this grows." Cria exotismo + uniqueness geográfica.

Compliance: 🟢 passa se for real. Sardinian bitter honey = real botanical (corbezzolo tree, Sardinia). Ikarian honey = real (Ikaria, Greece). ❌ NUNCA inventar geografia.

SlimSoda
"The baking soda that works isn't Arm & Hammer. It's Sardinian mineral-balanced, harvested from a specific lagoon."
Cardio Clear
"Sardinian Bitter Honey comes from the corbezzolo tree. It only grows on one island in the Mediterranean."
Linfozen
"Brazilian Pineapple stem — only the specific bromelain-rich variety from the southern highlands dissolves lipedema fat."
Regra de ouro: geografia real = ok. "Rare Himalayan herb" sem LOCATION específica = 🟡 borderline. "Ancient remedy" vago = 🟢 passa (não é claim).
6

Scarcity de Saúde

"Every day you wait, more cement hardens" · "Your capsule keeps thickening"

Loss aversion anchor: "If you don't act, your health gets measurably worse." Cria fear of deterioration temporal.

Compliance: 🟡 borderline. ✅ "The protocol takes 90 days" + ✅ "Inaction has a cost" = passa. ❌ "You will die" / ❌ "Your BP will kill you" = capability claim + mortality threat = Meta flag.

SlimSoda
"Every morning you wake up without the order, your metabolic switch stays on STORE. The bloat doesn't pause."
Cardio Clear
"In the next 90 days, if you do nothing, the cement keeps hardening. 5 more grams of plastic per week."
Linfozen
"If you don't do anything for the next 90 days, the fibrous capsule keeps hardening. More nodules form."
Regra de ouro: "Every day" / "Next 90 days" = temporal loss = 🟢 ok. "You will have a heart attack" = ❌ capability claim falso. "Talk to your doctor" + loss aversion together = best combo.
7

Scarcity de Dinheiro

"$20K liposuction vs $147 90 days" · "$1,000/mo injections vs $30 B1G1"

Loss aversion anchor: "If you don't act, you either pay 100× more or you keep paying monthly forever." Cria money comparison shock.

Compliance: 🟢 passa se preço público. ASPS 2024 = $5,000-10,000 average lipo. Real published numbers = ok. ❌ NUNCA inventar "industry charges $X" sem source.

SlimSoda
"The celebrity capsule is $100. The injections are $1,000 a month. The recipe is $29.99 for two tubs."
Cardio Clear
"20,000-dollar liposuction, where the fat just comes back. Two capsules. 30 seconds. 90 days. $294."
Linfozen
"Twenty-thousand-dollar liposuction, where the fat just comes back. It's wrong. It's expensive. Three ingredients. Costs less than a cup of coffee."
Regra de ouro: "1% the cost" / "less than a coffee" = ok (relative). "$20K vs $147" = ok (preço público). "Industry charges $X" sem source = 🟡.

🎯 Como mixar 2-3 tipos por copy (decision matrix)

Maria Soco (cold high-intent):
Mix 1+3+6+7 — scarcity produto + info + saúde + dinheiro. Agressivo. 4 tipos simultâneos.
Maria Quiet (warm exhausted):
Mix 3+4 — info + expertise. Curiosidade + autoridade. Sem 1, 2, 6, 7 (não grita). Calming close.
Premium publisher (CBS Health):
Mix 3+4+5 — info + expertise + geográfica. Editorial. Sem 1, 2, 6, 7 (L1).
Black hat extreme:
Mix 1+2+6+7+conspiração — todos os tipos. Mortalidade + urgency + money anchor.

🔗 Cross-project: como aplicar a QUALQUER copy DR

  1. Identifica o mix ideal pro avatar (Maria Soco vs Quiet vs premium publisher)
  2. Escolhe 3-5 tipos dos 7 baseado no mix (não use só 1, não use todos 7)
  3. Escreve 3 exemplos por tipo (1 EN + 1 PT-BR + 1 Meta-safe adaptation)
  4. Verifica compliance de cada (per red line + per tipo-specific rule)
  5. Testa em volume (Bifi P1 com 5 concepts × 3 texts usando mixes diferentes)
  6. Mede qual mix converte (Hook Performance Tracker)
  7. Itera o mix (substitui tipo que não converte por outro)
