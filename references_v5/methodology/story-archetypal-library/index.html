Story Archetypal Library · 15 Stories

Catálogo de 15 story archetypals que funcionam em QUALQUER vertical DR (suplemento, beleza, finanças, info-produto). Cada story tem: hook verbatim (EN + PT-BR), produtos onde funciona, mecanismo psicológico, red line check. Cross-project: replicável pra qualquer avatar com dor crônica + identity cost.

🎯 Por que story archetypals funcionam: cérebro humano é machine de pattern recognition. Stories que replicam estruturas familiares ("mãe lutando", "irmã que morreu", "filha comprando pra mãe") ativam empatia instantânea. 3 categorias canônicas: (1) Origin (quem fez, por quê) · (2) Mortality (quem morreu, quem quase morreu) · (3) Identity (perdeu algo importante, recuperou). Cross-vertical: o mesmo archetype funciona em qualquer vertical — muda só o setting.

📚 As 15 stories catalogadas

A1

"I made this for my sister" (Origin · Origem + sacrificio)

Sister archetype · Formulator · Personagem COM nome + cidade

Mecanismo: origem pessoal do formulator. "I made this for my sister Rachel. Three kids, a husband, a body she didn't recognize." Sem promoção, sem BS. Family motivation = highest trust signal.

"I made this for my sister Rachel. Three kids, a husband, a body she didn't recognize. She got dressed in the closet so her husband wouldn't see her. She had stopped being in photos with her own kids. I judged my own sister. I was wrong. Not a little wrong. Completely, shamefully wrong."

Produtos onde funciona: SlimSoda (Dana Whitfield → sister Rachel) · Linfaflow (Markus → mother) · MemoPryl (Markus → father) · qualquer formulator com story de família real.

A2

"Mr. Roberts didn't make it" (Mortality · Morte anunciada)

Mortality archetypal · Personagem FALECIDO (não narradora) · 64-year-old archetypal

Mecanismo: mortality story. Personagem morre ANTES de um evento emocional (granddaughter's recital). Identity cost = widow.

"Mr. Roberts, 64, didn't make it to his granddaughter's recital. He was on the pill for 12 years. His cardiologist knew why. The industry kept it quiet."

Produtos onde funciona: Cardio Clear (canonical) · Linfaflow (Margaret 62 blood clot) · MemoPryl (Goldie deepfake — ILÍCITO) · qualquer produto crônico BP/cardio/cognição com avatar 60+.

A3

"I bought this for my mom" (Proxy-Buy · Filha comprando pra mãe)

Filha 35-55 comprando pra mãe 70+ · "I sent her a bottle" · Sister-witness contrast

Mecanismo: proxy-buy. A filha 35-55 é quem Google "mom X help" às 2am. Ela é a compradora + advocate + witness. Mãe é o consumidor final.

"I'm 38. My mom is 67. She's had this since before I was born. She has spent $14,000 on procedures. Last month I sent her a bottle. Today she sent me a photo. They're different. They're not cured. But they're different."

Produtos onde funciona: SlimSoda (Pam 62 daughter) · Cardio Clear (Linda 55 daughter, Patricia 67) · MemoPryl (Linda 64 daughter) · Linfozen (Patricia H. mother-daughter).

A4

"I went quiet at the barbecue" (Confessional · Culpa/Redenção)

Maria confessional · "I made Kristin the villain" · Sister-witness beat

Mecanismo: confession of having been WRONG. Avatar que tentou de tudo + julgou outras que também sofriam. Redenção = produto.

"I made Kristin a villain in a story I had made up. It was a backyard barbecue last June. Kristin was three months postpartum. She picked up a hamburger. I said, without thinking, the thing every woman in America has heard a thousand times: 'Have you tried eating less?' She went quiet. She put the plate down. She didn't speak to me for three weeks."

Produtos onde funciona: SlimSoda (Maria 47 → Kristin) · Linfaflow (sister dynamic) · Linfozen (sister with lipedema).

A5

"My mom called at 7am" (Family POV · Mãe acreditando)

Mãe/filha · "I believe you, baby" · Long-distance support

Mecanismo: family belief when no one else did. Identity affirmation. "She didn't fix it. She just said she believed me."

"My mom called me one night, the way moms do when they have been biting their tongue for months. 'Maria, are you sure you are not sick? You used to eat whatever you wanted.' I sat on the kitchen floor and cried. 'Mom, I am eating less than I ever did. I just do not know what to do.' She went quiet. Then she said the only thing a mom can say from a thousand miles away. 'I believe you, baby.'"

Produtos onde funciona: SlimSoda (Maria → mom) · qualquer produto com avatar mulher que tem mãe viva acreditando nela.

A6

"I cried in the doctor's office" (Survivor contrast · Superacao)

Sobrevivente vs mortality (A2) · Emocional anchor · "I was wrong about supplements"

Mecanismo: survivor testimony. Contrast direto com archetype mortality (A2). Emoção = "I cried because I realized I was wrong about everything".

"My blood pressure was 190/130 when I started. My doctor had me on Lisinopril for 8 years. After 6 weeks on the protocol, my reading was 120/80. I cried in his office. I was completely wrong about the supplements my daughter kept trying to give me."

Produtos onde funciona: Cardio Clear (Patricia 67) · SlimSoda (Maria 47 post-protocol) · Linfaflow (Margaret sister post-protocol).

A7

"The closet where you get dressed" (Habit / Escondido) (Identity cost)

Esconderijo físico · "She got dressed in the closet" · Specific sensory detail

Mecanismo: physical hiding as identity cost. Specific location ("the closet") = sensory detail that makes it real, not abstract.

"She got dressed in the closet so her husband wouldn't see her. She had stopped being in photos with her own kids."

Produtos onde funciona: SlimSoda (Rachel archetypal closet) · Linfaflow (compression stockings hidden under long pants) · Linfozen (skirts abandoned, leggings only) · Cardio Clear (cardigan in summer to hide sweat).

A8

"My daughter drew a family picture and forgot me" (Identity · Filha/Esquecimento)

Filha esqueceu mãe · Identity erasure · "I wasn't in the picture"

Mecanismo: identity erasure. A criança (4-7 anos) desenhou família sem incluir a mãe. Custo identity máximo pra mãe.

"My niece drew a family picture at school last week. She drew my sister. She drew her dad. She drew her brother. She forgot to include her own mother. The teacher asked who the woman in the corner was. 'That's not anyone,' she said. 'That's just where mom usually stands.'"

Produtos onde funciona: SlimSoda (Maria 47 → niece) · Linfozen (Lisa 39 → daughter) · Cardio (Patricia → granddaughter identity win) · qualquer avatar mãe com identity cost de "ser esquecida".

A9

"The photo I'm not in" (Identity · Fotos evitadas)

Foto de família · "I'm never in photos" · Self-erasure from frame

Mecanismo: self-erasure from family photos. Specific event (wedding, birthday, Christmas) that the avatar avoids being photographed.

"We took a family photo at my daughter's wedding. I was behind the camera. I was the one taking the photo. I wasn't in any of them. My husband asked why. I said 'someone has to be behind the camera.' That was a lie. The truth was I didn't want to be seen."

Produtos onde funciona: SlimSoda (Maria 47) · Linfaflow (5pm sensation → avoids evening events) · Linfozen (skirts abandoned) · Cardio (Patricia → fear of being seen as "sick old lady").

A10

"My husband asked when I'd be back" (Future Pacing · Loss of partner)

Marido perguntou · "When will you be back?" · Loss of identity como spouse

Mecanismo: spouse witness to the avatar's transformation. Husband notices she's not the same. Loss of identity as "the woman he married".

"My husband asked me one night, 'When are you coming back?' I didn't know what he meant. He said, 'You've been gone for two years. The woman I married disappeared somewhere in 2024. I want her back.' I cried in the bathroom for an hour. Then I ordered the protocol."

Produtos onde funciona: SlimSoda (Maria 47) · Linfaflow (Margaret 62) · qualquer produto com avatar mulher casada established.

A11

"The receptionist looked at my chart twice" (Medical gaslighting · Sistema)

Sistema médico gaslighting · "You're too young for this" · 4 doutores que erraram

Mecanismo: medical gaslighting. Avatar que viu 4 doutores que erraram. Identity cost = "I knew my body, they didn't".

"I saw 4 doctors. The first said 'obesity.' The second said 'age.' The third said 'eating too much.' The fourth said nothing — he just looked at my chart twice and referred me to a therapist. The therapist was the one who said, 'Have you heard of lipedema?' I cried. Not because I was sad. Because someone finally said the name of the thing I had been carrying for 16 years."

Produtos onde funciona: Linfozen (4 doutores erraram) · Linfaflow (water pill forever) · MemoPryl ("Alzheimer's is genetic, nothing to do") · SlimSoda (4 doutores, all wrong).

A12

"The cardiologist who knew why kept it quiet" (Conspiracy · Industria)

"Industry kept it quiet" · Anti-establishment · Reason why oculto

Mecanismo: medical industry conspiracy declared. "We were treating the wrong thing" = cardiologist admission. Reason why for "this video gets taken down".

"His cardiologist told his wife the thing doctors don't usually say out loud: 'We were treating the wrong thing.' The industry kept it quiet. Not because they're evil. Because the alternative — admitting we spent 30 years treating symptoms instead of causes — is too expensive. Literally. Billions of dollars in statin prescriptions, BP medications, diuretics. That's not a market you disrupt."

Produtos onde funciona: Cardio Clear (canonical) · Linfozen ($4B lipo industry) · SlimSoda (Big Soda lobbied baking soda out) · MemoPryl ($100B Alzheimer's industry).

A13

"The procedure that didn't fix it" (Procedural failure · 4 tentativas)

Procedimento caro falhou · 4 tentativas · "I spent $X and nothing worked"

Mecanismo: previous solution failed. Avatar já gastou muito com alternativa cara. Identity cost = "I trusted the wrong people".

"I spent $14,000 on liposuction in 2022. The doctor said 'this will fix it.' It came back in 18 months. I spent $300 a week on manual drainage for two years. Nothing changed. I tried the injections for 6 months. $6,000. The fat came back worse. Then I tried acupuncture. Then a Brazilian diet. Then a Russian diet. Then I stopped diets altogether and gained 12 pounds. Then my daughter sent me this."

Produtos onde funciona: Linfozen (canonical) · SlimSoda (multiple diets failed) · Linfaflow (vein procedure Margaret) · Cardio (3 medications).

A14

"I had a mini-stroke" (Post-event · Frank 61 TIA)

Post-TIA / Post-event · "Scared me into action" · Last warning

Mecanismo: post-event trigger. Avatar teve evento (mini-stroke, cancer scare, hospitalization) que mudou prioridades. "I should have done this 10 years ago".

"Frank, 61. I had a mini-stroke 4 months ago. The TIA scared me into action. BP went from 175/105 to 128/82 last week. Cardiologist is cautiously optimistic. Bring this to your doctor — don't do what I did and wait for the warning sign."

Produtos onde funciona: Cardio Clear (canonical Frank 61) · SlimSoda (post-diagnosis) · Linfaflow (post-coágulo family member) · Linfozen (post-surgery failed).

A15

"I'm a nurse and I never heard of this" (Authority converted · Skeptic MD)

Skeptic profissional · "I'm an MD/RN and I doubted" · Converted witness

Mecanismo: authority figure converted. Skeptic profissional (MD, RN, pharmacist) que testou e foi convertido. Highest trust signal possível.

"I'm an internal medicine doctor, 18 years. I do not recommend supplements to my patients in general. I made an exception for 3 of them based on the protocol. All 3 showed meaningful BP reduction at 90 days. I am watching the others closely."

Produtos onde funciona: Cardio Clear (Dr. Michael H. 18 years) · Linfozen (Karen 52 RN) · SlimSoda (nurse aunt archetype) · MemoPryl (skeptic MD comment).

🎯 Como usar essa library (workflow)

  1. Briefing novo → Escolhe 2-3 archetypal stories baseado no avatar
  2. Combina com Path 1/2 variant (Identity + Mortality + Loss) → close estruturado
  3. Adapta verbatim pro produto + nicho (muda setting, mantém estrutura)
  4. Testa em volume (Bifi P1 com 5 concepts × 3 texts)
  5. Mede qual story convert (Hook Performance Tracker + story-archetype column)

🔗 Cross-project: como aplicar a QUALQUER copy DR

  1. Identifica o avatar (idade, gender, family status, chronic pain area)
  2. Pega 2-3 archetypal stories da matrix que funcionam pro seu produto
  3. Adapta verbatim (muda setting: nome, cidade, idade, doença — mantém estrutura)
  4. Combina com Path 1/2 (story abre, variant fecha)
  5. Testa 5 stories diferentes em Bifi P1 (5 concepts × 3 texts = 15 ads)
  6. Mede qual story converte (Hook Performance Tracker + story column)
  7. Promove winners pra P3 escala (a story que converteu)
