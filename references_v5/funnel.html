Post-Purchase

⚠️ Not Captured

Capturado (parcial): URL pattern /funnelish/thankyou?store=X&funnelishId=Y&cart={cartId}&key={key} identificado. Sem cart válido, server retorna "Not Found — The requested cart or store could not be found" (screenshot em pages/funnelish-checkout/thankyou-notfound-2026-08-16.jpg).

Esperado (não capturado — requer purchase real):

• Thank you page (confirmação de pedido, número de tracking, ETA entrega)

• Upsell / OTO (one-time-offer — geralmente 1-click bump tipo "Add a 2nd bottle at 50% off")

• Email sequence (7d, 14d, 30d check-ins)

• Subscription offer (monthly refills?)

Padrão típico: Thank you → OTO upsell ($39 bundle) → main offer confirmation. Email 1 imediatamente com tracking info.
