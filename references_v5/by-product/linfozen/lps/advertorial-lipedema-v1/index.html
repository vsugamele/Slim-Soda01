Can I take Linfozen if I'm on blood thinners?
Bromelain in high doses may have mild anticoagulant properties. If you are on Warfarin, Eliquis, Xarelto, Plavix, or any prescription blood thinner, talk to your doctor before starting Linfozen. For most healthy adults, Linfozen at the recommended dose is well-tolerated.
What if I'm already on Ozempic or Wegovy?
Linfozen does not interfere with GLP-1 agonists. Many women take both. However, if you are considering stopping Ozempic or Wegovy, talk to your doctor first — do not stop a prescribed medication without medical supervision. The two work on different mechanisms (visceral fat vs lipedema fat).
Will Linfozen interfere with my compression stockings or MLD sessions?
No. Linfozen is designed to work alongside compression therapy, manual lymphatic drainage (MLD), and other lipedema management strategies. Many women in our observation study reduced their MLD frequency over time — but always under the supervision of their lymphedema therapist or vascular physician.

Most women in our 200-person internal observation study reported a subjective improvement in leg heaviness and temperature within 2-3 weeks. Visible changes in dimpling and softness typically appeared between weeks 6 and 12.
