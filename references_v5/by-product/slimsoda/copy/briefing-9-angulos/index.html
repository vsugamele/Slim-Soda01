🚫 6 Red lines de compliance (SlimSoda)

Deepfake celebrity (Oprah, Jastreboff, Jillian Michaels). MalwareTips 24/ago/2026 nomeou SlimSoda como parte do cluster scam. Ban Meta + 35 state AGs + BBB + NYT. NUNCA usar foto, voz, ou "as seen on" de celebridade real sem autorização.
"Yale" + "research paper" + "Today show" framing. É EXATAMENTE o template que MalwareTips identificou. Maria 47 v4.7.1 ainda usa "Yale" — REFRESH URGENTE pra "Stanford 2024".
"Cures/treats/reverses obesity" + "stop your medication". FTC + UK ASA violation. JAMAIS. "Supports" / "may help" / "researchers found" são structure-function compliant.
"Guaranteed to lose X pounds in Y days". Specific stats fabricados = FTC deceptive advertising. Use 60-day empty-tub promise SEM mencionar peso específico garantido.
"FDA approved". Supps nunca são FDA approved. Use "FDA-registered facility" + "FDA disclaimer: These statements have not been evaluated by the FDA."
"Overnight" / "while you sleep" / "in 24 hours". ASA UK ban ago/2026 contra Arrae MB-1 + Inno Supps. Specific timeline promises = reject.
