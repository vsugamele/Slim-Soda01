🛑 Red lines Linfaflow (CRÍTICO)

  • "Cure lymphedema" / "cure for swollen legs" (red line crítico)
  • "Replace your water pill" (red line crítico)
  • "Vein procedure is unnecessary" (red line — ad Margaret diz "cancelled" mas não "unnecessary")

✅ SEMPRE:

  • "Talk to your doctor before changing your routine"
  • "30-day money-back guarantee"
